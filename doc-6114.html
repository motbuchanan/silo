RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

Rules of Engagement

1. Joe always knows the game is a game. If he ever seems genuinely rattled rather than delighted, flag it and we throttle back. Fun is the mission objective.

2. Your involvement stays secret until the finale. If Joe asks you directly about the app, you're mildly curious and a little skeptical. That's it.

3. Everything checkable stays true. Real programs (BLUE BOOK, STARGATE) stay factual — our fiction lives in the gaps between real things.

4. Drops go in public, safe, legal spots. No trespassing, for you or for him.

5. Nothing about real people we know goes into the game without a green light from Mot.

6. When in doubt, do nothing and ask. A missed beat is recoverable. A blown secret isn't.
