HOW TO USE THIS

SILO · CROP CIRCLE TRACKER
1 · NEW REPORTS

New crop circles show up as cards at the top. Tap OPEN RECORD to see where it is and what is known about it.

2 · PHOTOS AND VIDEOS

Inside any record, tap PHOTOS to see pictures of that formation, VIDEOS for drone footage and documentaries, and READ MORE for articles. GOOGLE EARTH shows the actual field from above.

3 · ALL FORMATIONS

Scroll down for every crop circle on file, with a satellite picture of each field. Use the search box to find one by name, place, or year.

4 · THEORIES & RESEARCH

The bottom section covers the big questions: who or what makes them, the hoaxers, the ones nobody has explained, and the researchers who studied the plants and soil.

5 · REPORT A SIGHTING

Seen something in a field yourself? Tap REPORT A SIGHTING under the new reports. Tell us what and where. It goes to the review queue and shows up in the registry if it checks out.

Status codes: LOGGED confirmed. UNVERIFIED one report only. UNDER REVIEW being looked into. WITHDRAWN removed from the list.
