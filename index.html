OPERATING INSTRUCTIONS · SILO REGISTRY TERMINAL
1 · THE MAP

Each marker is a logged formation. Pinch to zoom the satellite imagery down to field level. Tap a marker, then OPEN RECORD for the full registry entry. Red-ringed markers are new intakes.

Coordinates are given to four decimal places so that any entry can be independently checked against public satellite imagery and against the external archives listed in section 6. Historic intakes reach back to 1966 and span six continents; the registry is not a UK-only instrument, whatever the distribution suggests.

2 · THE REGISTRY

The list below the map holds every active record: reference number, designation, and status. Tap any row for date, region, formation type, coordinates, and survey notes.

3 · SEARCH

The search field filters by reference, name, region, type, or any word appearing in survey notes. Note that the registry index is larger than the default view.

4 · ALERTS

The bell lights when a new formation enters the registry. Data refresh runs daily at 0400 UTC. Tap an alert to jump to the record and its map position.

4b · INSTALLATION

Install SILO to your home screen when prompted. The registry then works without a connection and intake alerts can reach you directly. Enable alerts when asked, or you will only see new intakes when you happen to open the terminal.

5 · FIELD VISITS

GET DIRECTIONS routes to the formation from your position. Most formations sit on private farmland: view from public rights of way, and never enter a crop without the landowner's permission. Harvested fields close for the season.

6 · CROSS-REFERENCE SOURCES (EXTERNAL · UNAFFILIATED)

The registry is a filing instrument, not a primary source. Every record carries an EXTERNAL DOCUMENTATION block at the bottom of its entry: aerial imagery, archive listings, encyclopedia entries, and open discussion. Use them. A record that cannot be corroborated outside this system should be treated with more interest, not less.

General archives:

cropcircleconnector.comPrimary reporting archive since 1995. Most UK intakes originate here.
circleresearcharchive.comHistorical research database, free access.
bltresearch.comPlant and soil analysis literature (node elongation studies).
cropcirclecenter.comLarge formation archive with year indexes.
oldcropcircles.weebly.comPre-1980 historical reports.
r/cropcirclesOpen discussion and same-day sighting chatter.
7 · STATUS CODES
LOGGED — verified intake, record complete
UNVERIFIED — single-source report, retained pending review
UNDER REVIEW — active survey, record may change
RECLASSIFIED — origin established after intake; record retained as a control case
SEALED — access restricted by program office
WITHDRAWN — removed from the active index. Withdrawn references are not listed. They are, however, still numbered.
